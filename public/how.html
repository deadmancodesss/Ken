How it works

One sentence, three possible trips

Which trip you get depends on one thing: whether your memory already knows the address in front of you. The chain is the slow part, and the paid index is the only part that costs anything. Tap a bar to switch trips.

nothing spent time only, no money a cent or three, once crossed out, never asked

the ranges are wall clock, measured one sentence at a time against a live model. The number the model itself takes is the one that moves when a provider is busy.

The four workers

Take one away and a specific thing stops working

That is the honest test of whether a product needs a service. None of these four is decoration, and there is no fifth because nothing broke when we left one out.

Tatum the bird · eyes

Reads balances, transfers and counterparties on mainnet and testnet with one key per network.

without it you type addresses in, and the agent describes your imagination.

Walrus the hedgehog · memory

One namespace per wallet, holding plain sentences about people and what they cost.

without it every question re-reads a rate limited chain, and nothing is ever learned.

Gemini the frog · brain and mouth

Turns a sentence into a tool call, and a tool result into two spoken sentences.

without it you have a block explorer with extra steps.

x402 the mouse · the market

A price in a standard 402 header, paid by transfer bytes your wallet signed and the seller settles without holding a key.

without it the agent cannot find out anything new, and cannot pay a person for it.

Seen from inside

Four lines, and only one of them carries anything

A warm question never plugs into the chain. A question the chain cannot answer never plugs into the market until you say so. The empty jacks are the build.

The memory, exactly

A sentence, and the address that owns it

Nothing about this is clever storage. A fact is a line in your namespace and the name is the part a later question can find. That is what makes the second asking free, and what lets the same memory work in the app, on the call, and anywhere else that reads your wallet.

Your people are not another person's people, so the namespace is derived from the wallet you are speaking for. The masthead chip is the only way to move between them.

a paid line records its own price and its own transaction, so any memory can be explained back to you.

The safety rules

Five things this build refuses to do

Each refusal exists because the shortcut was available and would have made the demo better looking.

1

No key of yours on the server

The page prepares bytes and waits. Refusing to sign leaves nothing behind.

2

No memory written on a claim

A browser reporting a digest is not evidence. Both sides of the transfer are re-read from the chain.

3

No second payment for one answer

A receipt is spent the moment it releases a fact, and a payment with no fact is offered back.

4

No silent pricing

Amount, asset and payout arrive before anything is signed, and the running total is in the masthead.

5

No invented people

If memory has nothing and the chain cannot support a sentence, the agent says it does not know. A profile is measurement, not a made up business name.

What is still rough

The parts a judge should not be sold on

Nothing here is hidden inside the fold. Each line is a limit with a route, and the measurement behind it is one click away.

The brain borrows a fast modelcapacity
Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite leads and the 550B Nemotron Ultra is the second attempt, with Gemini 3.5 Flash and then the 4B Bolt behind them. The providers alternate so the second ask goes to a different one rather than to a second name on the key that just refused. On the nine probes this build ships, measured 2026-11-04, Gemini sent 6 of 6 tool calls and answered 3 of 3 spoken questions at a 3.4 second median with an 11.6 second tail. Ultra sent the same 6 of 6 and 3 of 3 at 1.2 seconds with a 1.9 second tail, so the provider that answers first is the slower one and that is the trade this build chose. Gemini's free ceiling is near ten requests a minute and fifteen hundred a day, NVIDIA's about forty a minute, so the fallback is also the roomier lane. The rest fell away: DeepSeek flash needed 20 to 30 seconds for one word, Step 5 answered 500, and Gemma, GLM, GPT OSS and MiniMax never finished in time. The first NVIDIA key was refused by every inference call on 2026-11-03 while its catalogue still answered, and a catalogue that says yes to a junk key is not evidence, so the second key is the one on record. The model makes real function calls against this tool layer and answers in plain sentences. What is not production is the capacity. A demo is cheap and a crowd is not.
The spoken voice arrives after the sentencelatency
/speak genuinely calls a text to speech model, and a short sentence takes about two seconds from HeyGen, three from Fish Audio and five from Gemini, so the room waits for the whole line. Nothing is spoken until the whole answer exists. A live call is the other case: there the model streams audio as it talks and you can interrupt it, and it stops.
Three voices can answer, and the page says whichmeasured
Fish Audio is tried first because it is limited by five requests at once rather than by a count of sentences. HeyGen is second, through OpenRouter, and it is the voice written off twice before it was measured: it does not appear in OpenRouter's own model list, it refuses wav and mp3 and speaks only raw pcm, so this server reads the sample rate off the reply and writes the header itself. Fish is not on that route, whatever a middleman's catalogue implies, so the second door leads to a different mouth. Gemini is last and covers any sentence the others refuse. Every allowance here is observed rather than published, so read each number as a floor.
Cadence needs days, not codetime
"About every three days" is measured from real transfers on different days, and the seeder will not pay the same person twice in one UTC day. Until it has run for a few days the honest answer is "most recently today".
No real microphone has been exerciseduntested
The capture path, the transcript and the spoken reply are built, and the call sheet holds a session and writes what it hears back into the chat, but every recorded demo so far has been typed.
No wallet extension was installed for these runsuntested
The signature leg was driven by a script that does what an extension does with the same bytes, so the handshake is proven and the popup is not.
Memory has no deletewalrus
Walrus exposes remember and recall, so removing a name means writing a corrected line and living with the old one in the namespace. A receipt is filed under its transaction, so the same payment cannot be stored twice, but one that is already wrong stays wrong.
Warm answers still take a couple of secondshonest
The recall round trip is the floor, which is why the interface shows what it is doing rather than pretending.
