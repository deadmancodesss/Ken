03 · Why you can relax

Three promises that never bend

Even if you speak fast or make a typo, these three safety nets protect your wallet every single time.

Promise 01 · Your thumbprint only

Talking never moves a single cent

Saying "Pay Sana $5" only prepares a slip on your screen. Ken does not have your password or private key—money only moves when you tap Approve in your own wallet.

Promise 02 · Pay once, keep forever

Never pays twice for the same name

Looking up an unknown address costs 1 cent the very first time. The Hedgehog writes it in your notebook so every future question about that friend is $0.00.

Promise 03 · Real calculator math

AI never guesses your dollar totals

Your monthly statement and budget bar are added up by a strict calculator directly from your receipts—never estimated or made up by AI.
