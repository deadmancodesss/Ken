Who these people are

Five invented wallets doing ordinary things to each other

Sana, Dev, Tara, Imran and Ravi are not real. They are testnet wallets that have been sending each other small amounts on a schedule so the agent has a genuine history to notice. Everything below is the agent answering from that real history, with the same addresses and the same totals you would see on the memory page.

what a wallet shows you

A list of transfers, newest first, with hex addresses you have to recognise yourself.

A balance that tells you the total and nothing about why.

what this shows you

The person attached to the address, the rhythm you taught it once, and the one thing that is off.

A sentence you can argue with, and a running total of what each answer cost.

What changes across a week

the week without it

You scroll a transfer list trying to remember who 0x8f3a is.

You find the bill you split by opening the chat where it happened.

You notice the subscription renewal only after it lands early.

You let one small mystery debit go because looking it up is annoying.

the week with it

You ask once, out loud, and the name is there for the rest of your life.

Each person carries a usual amount and a last seen date, so the question becomes a sentence instead of a scroll.

Every answer says what it read and what it cost, so you can tell a memory from a purchase.

Money leaves only when your own wallet signs, and you can see the running total from any page.

one honest limit on this page. The cadence numbers are computed from the dates money actually moved, and the wallets behind these five people were funded today, so every_about_days is still blank for all of them. The rhythm in the answers above comes from what you taught the agent in a note, and the arithmetic joins it once the history spans more than a day.

What it will not do

Pay on a sentence

It prepares the transaction and stops. Your signature is the confirmation, and refusing leaves nothing written.

Guess a name

An unknown address is reported as unknown. A paid lookup is offered, not performed silently, and the price is shown before anything is signed.

Share your people

Notes live in a namespace derived from the wallet you speak for, so your Sana is not somebody else's Sana.

Hold your key

No secret of yours is stored on this server, and the demo wallet is a scripted stand-in whose only money is worthless dust on testnet.

read the machinery behind all of this on the how it works page, or just start talking to it.
