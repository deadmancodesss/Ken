What a search does before it answers

Four steps, none of which reads the chain. The slow part of this product is also the free part, and that is the whole reason the agent can answer about your own people without asking you for a cent.

tap a bar to step through them.

One the word you type

You write groceries, or say it into a call. The word is never compared against the text of a memory, which is why a wrong word still finds the right line.

Two the question becomes a position

Walrus turns the sentence into a place in a space where meanings that belong together sit close, so groceries, shopping and a note about somebody's weekly shop land in the same region.

Three every line you own is measured

That happens inside your own namespace, derived from the wallet in the masthead. The chain is not read, Tatum is not asked, and nothing costs money.

Four the nearest lines arrive

The ticks in the right-hand pane show how near each memory sat to your question. They are drawn instead of a percentage so nobody reads them as a grade.
