Where this payment actually is
Nothing has been asked

Ask for a payment on the Talk page and the agent builds the transaction here, then stops. There is no queue and no retry. If you never sign, nothing left your wallet and nothing was written down.

Held on this page

A complete Sui transaction, waiting in one slot. Asking for a second payment replaces this one rather than leaving two unsigned tickets stacked up for you to sign by accident.

In your wallet now

The extension, not this page, is showing the amount and the address. This server holds the bytes and never your key, so cancelling there is the same as cancelling here.

Being read off the chain

The digest your wallet reports is re-read from the network and checked for exactly that amount leaving your address and arriving at that one. This page does not get to decide that it worked.

Written to memory

Only after the chain agrees does the payment become a line on the Memory page. From then on the agent answers about it from Walrus, and the chain is not read again for the same question.

this panel moves with the real ticket on this page, and nothing else.

What this page does and does not do
1

The agent prepares, it does not send

The bytes on this ticket are a complete transaction. Nothing has left your wallet until you sign.

2

Your extension signs, or nothing happens

Cancel and the ticket empties. No memory is written, no money moves, and the agent is told you refused.

3

The chain decides whether it counts

The digest you report is re-read from the network. If the amount did not arrive at that address, the page says so rather than pretending.

4

A payment that bought a name is journalled

If money moves and the index does not hand the fact over, the next ask retries that same receipt instead of sending you after a second payment.
